### Hello there 👋

I'm an end-to-end AI builder focused on **evaluations, benchmarks, and verification** — making AI systems more rigorous and trustworthy. Currently Principal AI Engineer at [Curebase](https://www.curebase.com), bringing evaluation to AI in clinical trials.

My background spans neuroscience research (Yale, SRI International), data science, and software engineering. I believe evals are the weak link in AI development, and I'm working to change that.
